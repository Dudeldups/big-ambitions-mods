[h1]Vehicle Repainter[/h1]

Give your vehicle a fresh look without buying a new one. Vehicle Repainter adds a native-feeling [b]Repaint[/b] option directly to the existing Repair and Wash service at gas stations.

[h2]How it works[/h2]
[list]
[*] Drive a vehicle into a gas station's normal Repair and Wash service bay.
[*] Select [b]Repaint ($800)[/b].
[*] Use the familiar vehicle color picker to preview every available color live on your vehicle.
[*] Confirm to pay $800 and save the selected color, or cancel to restore the exact original paint without being charged.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Vehicle Repainter works with vanilla vehicles and compatible modded vehicles that use Big Ambitions' standard vehicle color system.

[h2]Details[/h2]
[list]
[*] Fixed repaint price: $800
[*] The selected color persists with the vehicle and save game.
[*] The confirmation button is disabled when you cannot afford the repaint.
[*] No continuous vehicle polling or background scene scanning.
[*] No additional mods are required.
[/list]
